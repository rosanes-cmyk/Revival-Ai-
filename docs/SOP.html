High Equity Lead Revival

Text Revival Campaign · Twin Home Buyer & Equity Track Inc.

What this tool does

It works your leads for you. For every lead it opens the contact in REI BlackBook, checks that it's safe and allowed to text, and — only if it passes every rule — sends the correct approved text message and records the result on the dashboard.

You watch it happen live, and at the end you save a clean report. You never type a message or pick who to text — the tool follows the rules on every single lead, the same way, every time.

i How it works — the journey of one lead

This is what the tool quietly does to every single lead, one after another.

  1. Finds the lead in REI

    It searches REI BlackBook by address, name, or phone and opens that contact.

  2. Checks it's allowed to text

    Is the property in California? Was it already texted this month? Is the contact opted out or tagged “do not text,” sold, listed, wrong number? If any of these, it skips — no text.

  3. Picks the right message

    It reads whether the lead belongs to EQT or THB and chooses that company's approved message — never the wrong one.

  4. Sends the text & confirms it

    It types and sends the approved text, then confirms it actually went out before marking it “Text Sent.”

  5. Records the result

    The outcome shows on the dashboard and is saved, so you get an accurate report and no one is ever texted twice.

The big idea You just load the leads and press Start. The tool does the searching, the safety checks, the correct message, and the record-keeping — for hundreds of leads — exactly the same way every time.

1 Download & install the app

One-time setup. Do this once per computer.

Download the installer from Google Drive:

⬇ Download Revival AI (Google Drive)

  1. Open the Drive link

    Click the button above (or paste the link into your browser), then click Download in the top-right of the Drive page.

  2. Run the installer

    Open the downloaded Revival AI Setup.exe. If Windows shows a blue “Windows protected your PC” box, click More info → Run anyway (it's safe — it's just not from the Microsoft store).

  3. Finish & open

    Let it install. You'll get a Revival AI icon on your desktop. Double-click it to open the tool.

💡 Each person installs their own copy Colleagues on different computers or WiFi can all install it from the same Drive link. It still all runs through the one REI account, so nothing conflicts and no lead is texted twice.

4 Run the automation

Set it up once, press Start, and let it go.

  1. Turn ON Live Sending

    Click 💤 Live Sending: OFF so it turns 🟢 Live Sending: ON. OFF = safe practice mode (checks everything but sends nothing). ON = real texts go out.

  2. Set the batch size & Auto-continue

    Set Texts per run to 100 and leave Auto-continue checked. It sends 100, pauses a moment, then automatically keeps going — 100 at a time.

  3. Press Start

    Click ▶ Start Live Automation. It works down the list on its own and keeps going batch after batch until every lead is done — or until you press Stop.

  4. Watch it live

    The cards at the top and the table fill in as it works. Green links open the contact in REI or the property on Redfin for a quick check.

  5. Stop whenever you want

    Click ⏹ Stop to end your shift. Progress is saved — next time you press Start it picks up the unfinished leads and skips everything already done.

5 What the results mean

Click any card at the top to show only those leads in the table.

Text SentThe approved text was confirmed sent to a clean, eligible lead.
Sold / ListedThe property is sold or listed — not texted.
Not Int. / Opt OutThe lead said not interested or opted out — not texted.
To Delete / BadWrong number, failed number, bad lead, or not found in REI.
Out of StateProperty is outside California — never texted. (REI link still shown.)
Why some leads seem to “disappear” A few leads the tool can't confirm cleanly (for example, it couldn't tell EQT from THB) are quietly held and re-checked automatically on the next run. Nothing was texted — they just try again later. That's why they aren't shown as their own category.

6 The safety rules (automatic)

These are always on — you don't have to do anything.

  • California only. Anything outside CA is skipped as Out of State.
  • Never twice in one month. A lead already texted this month is skipped.
  • Right company, every time. It reads whether the lead is EQT or THB in REI and sends that company's message — never the wrong one. If it can't tell, it holds the lead instead of guessing.
  • Respects opt-outs & bad tags. If REI shows the lead is opted out or shouldn't be texted, it won't send.
  • Only marks “Text Sent” when it really sent. If a send can't be confirmed, it's held for another try — never falsely marked.
The two approved messages (locked)

Twin Home Buyer: “Hi, this is Juan with Twin Home Buyer. You contacted us before about selling your home. Are you still interested? Reply YES or NO. Thanks!”

Equity Track Inc.: “Hi, this is Juan with Equity Track Inc. You contacted us before about selling your home. Are you still interested? Reply YES or NO. Thanks!”

7 The daily report

At the end of your shift, capture the numbers.

Click 📊 Daily Report. It opens right inside the tool and shows:

  • Today — only what was worked today (your shift's numbers), with a plain-English summary line.
  • This Month so far — the running total for the whole month.

Use 💾 Save Today or 💾 Save This Month to download a clean one-page PDF — it saves straight to your Downloads, no printer needed.

? Quick fixes

  • A login page appears. Just log in once in that window — it's remembered afterward.
  • Nothing sends. Check that Live Sending is ON (green). OFF is safe practice mode.
  • The browser closed by itself. The tool reopens it and keeps going on its own.
  • A lead shows “Text Sent” but you're unsure. Click 🔁 Re-verify Sent — it checks REI and fixes any that didn't truly send.
  • Want to re-check errored leads. Just press ▶ Start again — it re-works anything unfinished and skips what's done.
👥 Working with colleagues Everyone can have their own copy installed, even on different WiFi. Because the “already texted this month” rule and REI's own history are shared through the one REI account, no lead ever gets texted twice, even if two people run it at the same time.
