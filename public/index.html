📋 How leads are decided — who gets texted

Checked in order; stops at the first that applies.

  1. In California? No → ❌ Out of State
  2. Found in REI? (address → owner → phone → email) No → ❌ Lead NOT Found
  3. Contact readable? No → ❌ Lead NOT Found (never guesses)
  4. Sold or Listed? → ❌ Sold / Listed
  5. Bad-tag / opt-out scan (tags + notes + full chat history):
    • Do Not Automate → ❌ skip
    • Opt-out / Do Not Contact / DNC / STOP / unsubscribe / remove me → ❌ Opted Out
    • Low Equity / We're Passing / Not a Fit / Not Qualified → ❌ Bad Lead
    • Not Interested → ❌ Not Interested
    • Wrong Number → ❌ Wrong Number
    • 2+ bad tags → ❌ Bad Lead
  6. Active-deal tag? (Offer Sent / Appointment Booked / Follow up):
    • conversation within ~a month → ❌ Recent Contact (don't disturb an active deal)
    • cold ~a month+ → ✅ continue (re-engage)
  7. Last text failed / undelivered? → ❌ Failed Number
  8. Already sent the revival text? (anywhere in the REI thread) → ❌ Already Contacted
  9. Already texted this month? → ❌ Texted This Month
  10. Usable phone? No → ❌ Lead NOT Found
  11. EQT or THB clear? Can't tell → ⏸ held (never guesses company)
  12. ✅ Clean → text the correct company's approved message
✅ WILL be texted (all true): CA · found & readable · not sold/listed · no opt-out / do-not-automate / low-equity / passing / not-interested / wrong-number / bad tags · not an active deal with recent contact · last text didn't fail · not already sent & not texted this month · has phone · company is EQT or THB · Live Sending ON
❌ will NOT be texted (any one): out of state · not found · sold/listed · Do Not Automate · opted out/DNC/STOP · low equity / we're passing / not a fit · not interested · wrong number · bad lead · active deal with recent contact · failed number · already contacted · texted this month · no phone · company unclear
